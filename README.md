Email and SMS marketing platform built for e-commerce brands, unifying email, SMS, reviews and forms in one account. The Sendlane v2 REST API manages contacts, lists, segments, tags, custom fields, sender profiles, suppression lists and email and SMS consent, returns campaign and SMS performance and revenue reporting, and ingests e-commerce behaviour events such as product views, cart abandonment and orders placed to drive automations and revenue attribution.

**Areas:** Email Marketing · SMS Marketing · E-Commerce · Marketing Automation · Contacts · Campaigns · Segmentation · Consent Management · Suppression · Event Tracking · Revenue Attribution

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for Sendlane: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
